Why Zircon

Everything between you and playing just… works

Built for people who want to play modded Minecraft with friends — not spend their evening reconciling mod versions and crash logs.

One-click join

Pick a server and hit PLAY. Zircon resolves the exact Minecraft + loader runtime (Fabric, Quilt, Forge, NeoForge), registers your join, and drops you straight in — fullscreen.

Exact mod sync

Every launch reconciles your mods against the server's Bill of Materials — downloading what's missing and purging stale JARs automatically.

Hash-verified mods

Every JAR is verified against Modrinth (SHA-1) and CurseForge (fingerprints) before it ever touches your game directory.

Official Microsoft sign-in

Full OAuth2 + PKCE flow with your own Microsoft account. Zircon never sees your password, and silent refresh keeps your session alive.

Packs & shaders

Per-server shaderpacks and resource packs — applied automatically, with a one-time "enable shaders?" prompt the first time a server offers them.

Skins & 3D preview

Upload 64×64 PNG skins with a WebGL 3D preview, a history gallery, and Mojang fetch/upload integration — your skin everywhere you play.

How it works

Three steps. Then you're in.

Sign in with Microsoft

One click, in your browser. Zircon uses the official OAuth2 + PKCE flow — your password never touches the app.

Pick a server

Choose from your saved servers or the recommendations panel. Each server publishes its own Bill of Materials — the exact mods it runs.

Hit PLAY

The launcher resolves the runtime, syncs and verifies every mod, then auto-connects. You spawn inside a fully modded world, ready to go.

Security first

Designed like your account and server matter.
Because they do.

Zircon was built around one idea: modded Minecraft shouldn't mean sketchy downloads and shared credentials.

  • Your account, your rules. Microsoft OAuth2 with PKCE and silent token refresh. There is no offline/unauthenticated mode — you must own Minecraft to play online, and nobody can join as you.
  • Verified mods, always. Every download is staged and hash-checked against Modrinth and CurseForge before use. Mod files are reconciled and purged — nothing stale, nothing unverified.
  • Anti-intrusion join gate. Each join uses a short-lived, one-time ticket. Vanilla clients are disconnected with a clear message, and the ticket store is capped so a public endpoint can't be abused.
  • Self-hosted admin. BCrypt-hashed users with JWT-secured sessions, a generated first-run password, and session caches stored in your OS keychain — not in plain text.
EULA-respecting by design. Zircon bundles no Minecraft code or assets, requires a licensed account, and uses Mojang's official endpoints for downloads. This project is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
For server owners

Run a modded server your friends can actually join

The same technology powers the server side — so you publish, and players just play.

  • Publish a Bill of Materials. Your server publishes the authoritative mod list; clients install exactly what you run. No more "works on my machine".
  • Single-port multiplexer. One public port smartly routes both Minecraft traffic and HTTP — no firewall gymnastics, no port juggling.
  • Multi-instance & version switching. Isolated worlds, loaders, and mods per instance — and switching Minecraft/loader versions re-resolves every mod for compatibility.
  • Live console & player tools. Stream the console over WebSocket, run commands from the browser, and manage whitelist, ops, and bans — applied instantly on a running server.
  • Automated backups. LZ4-compressed tar snapshots on a schedule with retention pruning and one-click restore.
  • System telemetry. CPU, RAM, and disk stats with a live history graph in the admin dashboard.
  • Secure by default. BCrypt + JWT admin auth, generated first-run password, and a bundled single-page dashboard — no exposed third-party services.
  • Players arrive ready. Your community installs one launcher, signs in once, and every future update just appears — no modpack reinstall rituals.
  • Headless installer. The manager downloads and installs the server JAR for you — spin up an instance without a manual setup guide.
  • EULA-first. Instances won't start until the operator accepts the Minecraft EULA in the admin UI.
