Universal 1-Click Migration

Switching from CurseForge, Prism Launcher, Modrinth App, or ATLauncher? Zircon automatically detects your installed launcher profiles on disk. Select your instances and import them losslessly into isolated sandboxes in under 2 seconds.

  • Lossless Preservation: Keeps options.txt, keybindings, worlds, and custom textures.
  • 100% Local & Private: No external cloud conversion; runs entirely on your PC.
  • Instant Play: Automatically assigns optimal Java runtimes and memory flags.

4-Player Couch Co-op with Gamepads

Turn your PC or living room TV into a split-screen gaming console. Connect up to 4 gamepads (Xbox, PlayStation, or generic Bluetooth) and play Java edition side-by-side with your friends and family.

  • Native Gamepad Input: Smooth analog stick movement, triggers, and vibration support.
  • Automated Grid Arrangement: Clean 2-player or 4-player viewport window tiling.
  • Independent Profiles: Each quadrant loads its own character skin and inventory.

LAN Auto-Discovery & Offline Worlds

Playing with roommates or friends on the same Wi-Fi? Zircon discovers local servers automatically without typing IP addresses or forwarding router ports. Plus, build isolated offline single-player instances with dedicated RAM allocation.

  • LAN Auto-Discovery: Finds nearby games and ping latencies on your home network.
  • Adaptive Memory Slider: Allocate 2 GB to 32 GB RAM per instance with ease.
  • Automatic Java Resolver: Fetches verified Eclipse Temurin JDKs with zero manual path setting.
